**Telegram bot** (`src/lib/bot/README.md`). Supported locales are RU/UK only, and `TelegramChat.locale` defaults to `ru`.

- Production status: built, not launched. As last recorded (2026-10-05) the four bot migrations are not applied to production, `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` is not set and the webhook is not registered; unknown since then until the owner confirms (bot README, "Production status").
- Address lookup: a building-level answer, with a district fallback, and a city fallback below `MIN_AREA_REPORTS` (`lookup.ts`; the constant lives in `src/lib/cost-figures.ts`).
- District names are matched before geocoding (`districts.ts`). The typed-district card (`renderDistrict`) shows the groups with N from `getDistrictBySlug().reference` (`loadAreaReference`: the district from `MIN_AREA_REPORTS` reports, else the city); its rank line, `tg_district_checked.report_count` and the `/districts` table stay on `cost-baselines.ts` until Q-2.
- Deep links (`start-payload.ts`): `dep` starts the deposit questions, `b_<uuid>` opens a building's card, `d_<slug>` a district's card (saving nothing), `web_<source>` and any other payload are attribution only (`tg_bot_started.payload`, first-touch `TelegramChat.startPayload`).
- Saved interests (building, district, city waitlist) are stored as `TelegramSubscription` and listed by `/my`; nothing sends from them: no notifier exists.
- City waitlist for addresses outside Warsaw (`targetKey = "c:<slug>"`).
- Deposit-return flow (`deposit-flow.ts`, `deposit.ts`): five intake questions, a rule-based verdict (worded as the law's general rule, with no strength phrase, when the contract was not read or is not a lease or the reason is `other`; no verdict when more than the deposit was returned), a Polish demand letter, and two opt-in reminders (on the deadline, and 8 days after the letter) sent by the `telegram-followups` cron. Each due row is claimed before sending, so delivery is at most once; `/cancel` leaves the intake and clears pending reminder dates.
- Lease reading (`contract.ts`): Claude structured output with server-side fallback, run after the webhook response via `next/server` `after()`. Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` the contract step fails gracefully.

**Payments in code** (`src/lib/stripe.ts`, `src/lib/pricing.ts`):

- Products are the price IDs `EXTRA_LISTING`, `PROMOTE_7/14/30` and `COST_ACCESS_7/30/90`. `src/lib/pricing.ts` holds display-only PLN amounts, some still marked PLACEHOLDER.
- A listing beyond `FREE_LISTING_LIMIT = 2` active unpaid listings is created as `pending_payment` (`api/listings/route.ts:250-257`).
- Promotion is gated by the PostHog flag `promoted-listings-enabled`. The gate fails closed on the server (`api/checkout/listing/route.ts:70-79`) and also exists on the client (`create-listing/page.tsx`, `dashboard/client.tsx`).
- `/api/checkout/cost-access` has **no** flag gate. The UI only reaches it through the "renew" button, which renders only for users who already have paid access, because cost data is open to everyone (`components/costs/cost-access-card.tsx`).
- The webhook handles `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.expired` and `charge.refunded`. It sets `Listing.isPaid/isPromoted` or `Profile.costAccessUntil`.
- No subscriptions exist anywhere.

**`CostReport.source`** is set in `src/app/api/cost-reports/route.ts:385-388`. The system author ids are in `src/lib/import-constants.ts`.

- `user`: a normal submission, including anonymous ones. An anonymous report is owned by `ANON_AUTHOR_ID` and carries an `anonymousId` from the `pf_anon` cookie. It is re-pointed to the real account on first login (`src/lib/claim-reports.ts`).
- `import`: an admin "fill on behalf" entry (originally Google Form imports). It is owned by `IMPORT_AUTHOR_ID` and, when an `importedEmail` is given, auto-claimed on that email's login (`auth/callback/route.ts`).
- `scraped`: an admin entry from scraped listings such as Otodom. It is owned by `SCRAPED_AUTHOR_ID` and never claimed. `ADMIN_IMPORT_MODE` picks the admin mode.

How each source is counted:

- The blended cost medians (`cost-aggregates.ts`'s per-building median fields and `rollUpDistricts`, `cost-baselines.ts`) filter on `isVisible` only, so all three sources count. They feed the single-number surfaces not yet moved to D23 (city hub, district pages, calculator, home, blog report, OG, share page, bot `/districts`, neighbours: T052 – T054).
- The checker (`/api/location-score`, `summariseCosts`) answers with the building's `FigureSummary`, the `loadAreaReference` area (district from `MIN_AREA_REPORTS` reports, else the city, else none) and that area's `loadDepositReturns` statistic, all from the contract's loaders below. The page (`check/client.tsx`) renders them through `src/components/buildings/checker-figures.tsx`: the building's source line and groups with their N, one observation and the known part (the building page's helpers in `building-figures-view.ts`), then the labelled district or city reference and the area's deposit statistic from `MIN_DEPOSIT_ANSWERS` answers (`areaDepositMessage`), with no "cheaper / pricier than the district" conclusion; a successful in-city `address_checked` adds `answer_level`, `source_kind`, `n` and `incomplete` (`addressCheckedProperties` in `src/lib/analytics/checker-events.ts`). Its neighbour pins, like the bot's neighbours, keep one blended number per building (`aggregateLocationCheckerCosts` in `location-checker.ts`, a thin wrapper over the contract's `metricFigure`; a single-number surface frozen by Q-2).
- The shared figure contract (`src/lib/cost-figures.ts`, pure; spec 03) splits reports into a `tenant` group (`user` + `import`) and an `asking` group (`scraped`) with per-metric N, untrimmed medians and the `MIN_AREA_REPORTS` / `MIN_DEPOSIT_ANSWERS` gates. Its server loaders (`src/lib/cost-figures-query.ts`: `loadBuildingFigures`, `loadAreaReference`, `loadDepositReturns`) read visible reports only, are cached with `unstable_cache` under the `costs` tag (300 s, expired by `revalidateCostSurfaces()`), and call straight through where Next has no incremental cache (`npm run bot:dev` under tsx). Surfaces move to it task by task; until then the medians above stay as they are. The building page reads its figures from `loadBuildingFigures` and shows the two groups apart; the items it still computes itself (winter/summer, periodic charges, deposit, tenure, lease type) are in `src/lib/building-tenant-details.ts`, over the tenant group only; its display decisions (source line, one observation without a median or comparison, the known part of the costs, value / 0 / included in rent / unknown per breakdown row, dates as month and year and the tenancy as years, the neutral sample-size badge, and the no-report fallback `buildingFiguresFallback`: building figures, else the labelled district or city reference from `loadAreaReference`, else "no figures for this area" with the location score and the nuisances) are pure helpers in `src/lib/building-figures-view.ts`; the nuisances block is `src/components/buildings/nuisances-card.tsx`, shared with the checker; its comparison sets the building's tenant group beside the tenant group of the same `loadAreaReference` area, metric by metric with each side's N (`comparisonRows`), with no "cheaper / pricier" conclusion; a percentage, position bar or percentile needs `isComparable` (`cost-figures.ts`: same group, the same single rental type, N ≥ `MIN_COMPARABLE_N` on both sides), so none appears while the page loads every rental type. `src/components/costs/district-position-bar.tsx` is neutral (no zones, colours or badges).
- The post-submit screen (`POST /api/cost-reports` returns `comparison.own` and `comparison.reference`) and the dashboard's `CostReportsPanel` (`dashboard/page.tsx`) set the user's own report beside the tenant group of the `loadAreaReference` area (`ownReportReference` / `ownReportRows` in `src/lib/own-report-reference.ts`, rendered by `src/components/costs/own-costs-reference.tsx`): monthly total, rent per m² and total per m², each area figure with its N; an incomplete report's total is the known part and is set beside nothing; no overpay, saving, percentage or position bar (one report never passes `isComparable`). Their share links carry only the district (`?d=`). `cost-baselines.ts` remains for the share page and for the bot's `/districts` table and the typed-district rank line (Q-2).
- The bot's address answer (`lookupAddress` in `src/lib/bot/lookup.ts`, and `lookupBuilding` for the website's `/start b_<id>` link through the same internal `assembleLookup`, both read-only) reads `loadBuildingFigures`, `loadAreaReference` and the reference area's `loadDepositReturns`, with the checker's level rule (building with a visible report, else district from `MIN_AREA_REPORTS`, else the city); only its neighbours keep the blended number (Q-2), shown with its own N. `format.ts` renders the building and area cards like the checker (spec 04 §Cards on the shared contract): one block per group (tenant reports, then asking prices), the headline picked by `groupFigureRows` (`building-figures-view.ts`) with its N, one observation without a median or N, the known part labelled as such, and the district or city as a labelled reference line with its N; no "pricier / cheaper than the district" verdict. ## 4. Request flow and boundaries

- **DB access**: Prisma singleton `src/lib/prisma.ts`, cached on `globalThis` outside production.
  - Server components (20 page files under `src/app/[locale]`) and route handlers query Prisma directly. Client components never do; they call `/api/*`.
  - The one server action module is `src/app/[locale]/auth/actions.ts`.
  - The only Supabase data API (PostgREST) read is in middleware: `profiles.deleted_at` via the anon key, which depends on an RLS policy that allows a user to read their own profile (**unverified**). `src/lib/active-user.ts` repeats this check through Prisma for API routes.
- **Supabase clients** (`src/lib/supabase/`):
  - `server.ts`: cookie-bound, anon key; used by server components, route handlers and server actions.
  - `client.ts`: browser singleton for auth state and Realtime.
  - `admin.ts`: service role; auth admin and `generateLink`.
  - `broadcast.ts`: service role; Realtime `httpSend` pings, no message bodies.
  - `storage-server.ts`: service role; deletes from the `listing-photos` bucket.
  - Some routes build their own clients inline: `api/checkout/cost-access/route.ts`, `cron/cleanup-accounts/route.ts`.
- **Middleware** (`src/middleware.ts`):
  1. Runs next-intl locale routing.
  2. Sets the first-touch `pf_ref` cookie from `?ref=` (`src/lib/referral.ts`).
  3. Sets the `pf_anon` cookie only on `/costs/submit` and `/review` paths. Setting it on every page would put a Set-Cookie on every response and defeat the CDN/ISR cache.
  4. Runs a Supabase `getUser()` only for `/dashboard`, `/create-listing` and `/messages` (redirect to `/auth/login?next=<path + query>`, built by `loginNextPath` in `src/lib/safe-next-path.ts` under the `safeNextPath` rules: an unsafe query is dropped, an unsafe path gives no `next`) and for `/account-deleted`. Soft-deleted users are bounced to `/account-deleted`. All other pages skip the auth round-trip.

  The matcher excludes `/api`, `/_next`, `/_vercel` and files. `/admin` is not a protected path; the admin pages check `getAdminUser()` themselves.

- **Caching**: the cost surfaces carry no cookies or auth, so they can be ISR-cached.
  - Pages with `revalidate = 3600`: city hub, district page, building page, calculator.
  - `unstable_cache` entries tagged `costs`: `getBuildingsData` (300 s, `src/lib/cost-aggregates.ts`), city lookup on `/costs` (600 s), home district medians (300 s), the figure-contract loaders (300 s, `cost-figures-query.ts`).
  - Untagged caches: home stats, top building, about stats, `getCityWithDistricts` (`src/lib/listings-query.ts`, 3600 s).
  - `src/lib/revalidate-costs.ts`: `revalidateCostSurfaces()` expires tag `costs` (`{ expire: 0 }`) and every locale variant of the city, calculator, building and district paths. It is called from the cost-report POST/PATCH and the admin cost-report routes. `revalidateBuildingPage()` is called after tag votes. Neither function throws.
  - `force-dynamic` routes: costs share, admin, email unsubscribe, blog data report, `building-tags`, `location-score`, telegram webhook.
- **Rate limiting**:
  - `src/lib/rate-limit.ts` is an in-memory fixed window per serverless instance, not global. It is used by the auth actions, `/api/contact`, `/api/city-notify` and `/api/listings/[id]/translate`.
  - The checker has its own per-IP limiter (10/min) plus a DB-backed cap on new buildings (200/hour) (`location-checker.ts`).
  - Cost reports have a DB-count limit per submitter and a global one (`api/cost-reports/route.ts:238-262`).
  - The bot allows 12 messages/min per chat (`isRateLimited` in `bot.ts`).
- **Cron** (`vercel.json`, UTC). Every route is guarded by `requireCronAuth`, which fails closed when `CRON_SECRET` is unset (`src/lib/admin-auth.ts`).

| Path                               | Schedule      | Does                                                                                                                                                                       |

## 5. Analytics

- **Client** (`src/components/providers/posthog-provider.tsx`, `src/lib/analytics/posthog-client.ts`, `src/lib/consent.ts`):
  - `posthog.init` runs in `src/instrumentation-client.ts` (`initPostHog`), which Next.js executes once per full page load before React hydrates. No component effect can capture before it (spec 01 §Landing capture, FR-13). Before T002 it ran in the provider's own effect, after its children's effects, and posthog-js dropped those early captures: the landing `$pageview` on some pages, `auth_started` and other mount-time events. The provider only passes the client to `posthog-js/react`. `init` runs only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY` is set; the host defaults to `https://eu.i.posthog.com`. An `init` that throws is logged, and the page renders without analytics. `e2e/posthog-landing.spec.ts` checks this in a real browser, with a dummy key and a fake host answered by the test.
  - Capture is on before consent with `persistence: 'memory'`, which writes no cookie and no localStorage.
  - On `accepted` (the localStorage key `passflat-cookie-consent`), `PersistenceUpgrade` calls `set_config({ persistence: 'localStorage+cookie' })` and keeps the same identity. A decline stays in memory mode.
  - Session recording requires consent plus the flag `session-recording-sample`. Surveys are off until consent.
  - `$pageview` is captured manually and registers `ref` / `utm_*` once.
  - Page context is attached in the `before_send` option of `posthog.init` (`createPageContextHook` / `withPageContext` in `src/lib/analytics/page-context.ts`). At send time every event except `$snapshot` gets `page_type`, `locale`, `city` (and `listing_type` on listing pages) from `getPageContext(window.location.pathname)`, plus `entry_page_type`. They go under the event's own properties, so a property the event sends itself wins. `entry_page_type` maps the SDK's `$session_entry_pathname`, which posthog-js keeps per `$session_id` in its own persistence. An event without that property falls back to the pathname of the first event the hook saw for its `$session_id`, kept in module memory. Nothing new is stored.
  - Limitations: in memory mode (no consent) a full page load starts a new person and session, so the entry is lost on reload. In consented mode the memory fallback restarts the entry on reload; the SDK property survives a reload.
  - Feature-flag keys live in `src/lib/feature-flags.ts`.
- **Server** (`src/lib/posthog-server.ts`):
  - Exports `trackServerEvent`, `identifyUser`, `isServerFeatureFlagEnabled` (fails closed), `captureServerException` and `flushPostHog`. The client uses `flushAt: 1`, and routes that must not lose events call `flushPostHog()`.
  - `src/instrumentation.ts` sends `onRequestError` to PostHog.
- **Bot identity**: `src/lib/bot/analytics.ts` sends events as `tg_<chatId>` with `source: 'telegram_bot'`. It never joins the web identity, so the bot is a separate cohort. The check events' properties come from `src/lib/bot/check-events.ts` over the shared `answerProperties`. The Telegram webhook flushes PostHog in `after()`, and the follow-ups cron awaits the flush. A contract reading runs in its own `after()` task (`runInBackground` in `deposit-flow.ts`) and awaits `flushPostHog()` when it settles, since Next runs `after()` callbacks concurrently.
- **Event names** are inline string literals at each `capture(` / `trackServerEvent(` / `track(` call. There is no registry. `src/lib/bot/README.md` ("Events") lists every bot event with its properties.
- **Activation funnels** (PRD 1, E8 / E9): `docs/analytics/activation-funnels.md` holds the event table, the five funnels with their queries and windows, the database counts and the limitations. Update it when a funnel event, property or table changes.
- **Localhost**: `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY` is commented out locally (`CLAUDE.md`; agents do not open `.env.local`). Without the key both the client and server wrappers do nothing, so events cannot be checked on localhost. Test the capture call and confirm in PostHog Live Events after deploy. The e2e build alone sets a dummy key and the fake host `https://posthog.e2e.invalid` (`playwright.config.ts`): `e2e/posthog-landing.spec.ts` answers the browser's requests to it, nothing reaches PostHog, and the server wrapper's flushes to that host fail with `ENOTFOUND` in the e2e log by design.

## 9. Known limitations and stale areas

Each item is a fact checked in the named file unless marked otherwise.

1. The old `ARCHITECTURE.md` says Next.js 15; `package.json` pins `next` 16.2.6. It lists a `ua` locale and `ua.json`; the code uses `uk` (`src/i18n/routing.ts`).
2. Old URLs `/add-costs` and `/add-replacement` do not exist. The real paths are `/[city]/costs/submit` and `/create-listing`. The calculator is `/[city]/calculator`, not `/[city]/costs`. The city slug is `warsaw`, not `warszawa`, and there is no Berlin or `/de` route.
3. Old table names `replacement_listings` and `replacement_responses` are now `listings` (with `type`) and `listing_responses`. `ListingResponse`, `Subscription` and `CostTermLabel` have no Prisma reads or writes in `src/`.
4. The old `payments.product_type` values (`promoted_listing`, `cost_report`, `subscription`) are wrong. The code writes `listing` / `cost_access` (`api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts`), and Checkout runs in `mode: 'payment'` only.
5. Old stack entries that are not in `package.json` or the code: Trigger.dev, Vercel Flags, `src/lib/supabase.ts`, `src/lib/mapbox.ts`, `next.config.ts`, `tailwind.config.ts`, `src/styles/globals.css`, `public/locales`. Image resizing goes through Vercel Image Optimization (`next.config.mjs`), not edge functions.
6. The old map plan (a split-view listing browser) describes `components/map/MapView.tsx`, which only the frozen listings pages use. The cost map is `components/map/CostsMap.tsx` on `/[city]/costs`.
7. The old doc says `has_contributed_cost` unlocks cost data. Cost data is now open to everyone (`components/costs/cost-access-card.tsx`), and the flag is kept only for display.
