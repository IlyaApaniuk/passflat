**concluded on** `[DATE]` **in** `[CITY]`

between:

1. **The Outgoing Tenant (Assignor):**
   `[FULL NAME]`, residing at `[ADDRESS]`, holding ID document `[DOCUMENT TYPE AND NUMBER]`, PESEL `[PESEL]`,
   — hereinafter the **"Outgoing Tenant"**,

2. **The New Tenant (Assignee):**
   `[FULL NAME]`, residing at `[ADDRESS]`, holding ID document `[DOCUMENT TYPE AND NUMBER]`, PESEL `[PESEL]`,
   — hereinafter the **"New Tenant"**,

3. **The Landlord (Owner of the premises):**
   `[FULL NAME / COMPANY]`, residing / with registered office at `[ADDRESS]`, holding `[DOCUMENT TYPE AND NUMBER / NIP]`,
   — hereinafter the **"Landlord"**,

jointly the **"Parties"**.

> **Why three parties?** Transferring the tenant's entire position requires (i) an assignment of claims — art. 509 et seq. of the Civil Code, and (ii) an assumption of debt — art. 519 et seq. The assumption of debt is effective **only with the creditor's (Landlord's) consent**, which must be **in writing under pain of nullity** (art. 522 of the Civil Code). That is why the Landlord is a party and grants consent in this document.

## § 2. Subject matter (transfer of rights and obligations)

1. The Outgoing Tenant transfers to the New Tenant **all rights and obligations** under the Lease, and the New Tenant **assumes** them, as of `[TRANSFER DATE]` (the "**Transfer Date**").
2. As of the Transfer Date the New Tenant steps into the place of the Outgoing Tenant and becomes a party to the Lease on the existing terms.
3. The terms of the Lease (rent, term, payment dates, termination rules) **remain unchanged**, unless the Parties agree otherwise in a separate annex.

## § 3. Landlord's consent

1. The Landlord hereby **consents** to the transfer of all rights and obligations under the Lease from the Outgoing Tenant to the New Tenant, including the assumption of debt within the meaning of art. 519 et seq. of the Civil Code.
2. The consent covers the **named** New Tenant and is granted in writing.
3. The Landlord declares that the Lease **does not contain / contains*** a prohibition or restriction on transferring rights and obligations; any restrictions are described in Annex no. `[NO.]`.

## § 4. Settlements, rent and utilities

1. The Outgoing Tenant declares that as of the Transfer Date they **are not in arrears / are in arrears*** with rent and charges. Any arrears of `[AMOUNT]` PLN will be settled by `[WHO]` by `[DATE]`.
2. Rent and charges for the period **up to** the Transfer Date are borne by the Outgoing Tenant; for the period **from** the Transfer Date — by the New Tenant.
3. The Parties will transfer the utility contracts (electricity, gas, water, internet, building/cooperative charges) to the New Tenant within `[NUMBER]` days of the Transfer Date. Meter readings as of the Transfer Date are recorded in the handover protocol (§ 6).

## § 5. Deposit

The Parties agree the following deposit arrangement *(choose option A or B)*:

- **Option A** — The Landlord returns the deposit of `[AMOUNT]` PLN to the Outgoing Tenant, and the New Tenant pays the Landlord a new deposit of `[AMOUNT]` PLN by `[DATE]`.
- **Option B** — The New Tenant reimburses the deposit of `[AMOUNT]` PLN directly to the Outgoing Tenant, and the deposit previously paid to the Landlord remains as security and is treated as the New Tenant's deposit. The Landlord acknowledges and accepts this.

> Reminder: the deposit is returned within 30 days of vacating the Premises, after deducting the Landlord's claims (art. 6(4) of the Tenant Protection Act). The protocol (§ 6) protects the New Tenant from being charged for pre-existing wear.

## § 6. Handover protocol and delivery of the Premises

1. On the Transfer Date the Outgoing Tenant hands over the Premises with its equipment and `[NUMBER]` sets of keys to the New Tenant.

## § 7. Release of the Outgoing Tenant from liability

1. As of the Transfer Date the Landlord **releases the Outgoing Tenant** from obligations under the Lease arising after the Transfer Date.
2. The Outgoing Tenant remains liable only for obligations arising **up to** the Transfer Date.
