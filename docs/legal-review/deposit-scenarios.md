# Deposit helper: scenarios as implemented

> **Read this first.** This document describes what the code of the Telegram bot's deposit
> helper does, as of commit `a5972fa` (2026-11-19), updated for the general-rule wording of FR-43
> (task T042, owner decision D26, 2026-11-19) and for the informal «ты» / «ти» address form of
> every text (task T061, owner decision D42, 2026-11-19). It is **not legal advice** and it **has not
> been reviewed by a lawyer**. It adds no rule and changes none: where this document and the code
> disagree, the code is what runs and this document is wrong. Its purpose is to give a Polish
> lawyer one place to review every case the bot distinguishes, what the bot tells the person in
> each, and which statute the code cites for it (spec: `docs/specs/04-telegram-bot.md`, "Scenarios
> for a lawyer", FR-44).

## How to read this

- Code: `src/lib/bot/deposit.ts` (rules, deadline, letter), `src/lib/bot/deposit-flow.ts`
  (conversation, verdict rendering, buttons, follow-ups), `src/lib/bot/deposit-texts.ts` (bot copy),
  `src/lib/bot/contract.ts` (reading the lease). Citations are `file:line` at the commit above;
  `deposit.ts` alone means `src/lib/bot/deposit.ts`, and so on. T042 changed `deposit-flow.ts`
  and `deposit-texts.ts` after that commit, so their line numbers have moved; `deposit.ts` (rules,
  deadline, letter) did not change. What T042 added (section 2a, the general-rule rows, the split
  of each verdict line into a strength phrase and a rule explanation) is cited by function and
  key, without line numbers.
- The bot speaks Russian and Ukrainian. Its copy is cited by key (for example `ruleWear`); the
  Russian and Ukrainian texts sit under the same key in `deposit-texts.ts`. Line numbers point to
  the Russian text. This document gives an English rendering of each text in "Verdict texts" below.
  The demand letter is Polish and is quoted verbatim.
- Terms: tenant = najemca; landlord = wynajmujący; deposit = kaucja; handover protocol = protokół
  zdawczo-odbiorczy; demand letter = wezwanie do zapłaty; "uopl" = ustawa z dnia 21 czerwca 2001 r.
  o ochronie praw lokatorów; "KC" = Kodeks cywilny.
- The verdict is computed by fixed rules, not generated by a model (`deposit.ts:4-6`). Only the
  lease is read by a model (see "Known limits").

## 1. The conversation

Entry points: `/start dep` (`bot.ts:287-290`), the button under `/deposit` (`bot.ts:325-350`), and a
message that mentions a deposit (`залог`, `застав`, `kaucj`, `депозит`, `deposit`) and contains no
digit (`deposit-flow.ts:65`, `:459-466`). Starting a case abandons the chat's other open cases
(`deposit-flow.ts:357-362`). `/cancel` abandons an open intake and clears pending reminders
(`deposit-flow.ts:473-486`).

## 2. What decides the verdict

`assessDeposit` returns six values (`deposit.ts:237-252`):

- `claim` = deposit − returned, never below 0 (`deposit.ts:245`);
- `deadline` and `deadlineSource` (`statute` or `contract`) from `depositDeadline` (`deposit.ts:239`, `:247`);
- `overdue` = today's UTC date is after the deadline (`deposit.ts:241`);
- `days` = whole days between today and the deadline, either way (`deposit.ts:242`);
- `strength` from `assessStrength(reason, protocol, contract)` (`deposit.ts:213-235`).

The message also depends on the contract status, the clause quotes and the landlord kind read from
the lease (`deposit-flow.ts:172-225`). Apart from two dependencies (R3 and R4 end the case before
any verdict; D2 – D4 and the contract-clause rows of table S need a read contract, C4), every
combination of one row from each of tables R, C, D, T and S below is a scenario the bot can
produce, and no other input changes the verdict or the letter. Some combinations are general-rule
cases (section 2a), which change how the verdict is worded but not what it computes.

## 2a. General-rule cases

FR-43, owner decision D26 (2026-11-19): where the bot cannot apply the rules to this person's case,
it states the law's general rule and draws no conclusion about the person's position. The deadline
computation, the rules in `deposit.ts` and the letter are the same as in the other cases. A case is
a general-rule case when the contract could not be read or is not a lease, or the reason is
`other`; a returned amount above the deposit is also unsupported and gets no verdict at all.

| #   | Case                                               | What the verdict shows instead                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Code                                                               |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| G1  | Contract not read (C2)                             | Header `generalRuleHeader`; right under it `generalRuleContractFailed` ("the contract could not be read, so below is the general rule of the law, not a conclusion about your contract; the term in your contract may be different"); the rule explanation of table S without its strength phrase and mark (✅, ⚖️, ⚠️, ℹ️); `disputedSum` before the disclaimer. The deadline line, `contractNote`, `noProtocolNote` and the letter button stay. | `renderVerdict` (`contractIssue = 'failed'`), `processContract`    |
| G2  | Not a lease (C3)                                   | As G1 with `generalRuleNotLease` ("this does not look like a lease, so below is the general rule …") in place of `generalRuleContractFailed`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `renderVerdict` (`contractIssue = 'not_lease'`), `processContract` |
| G3  | Reason `other` (S12), whatever the contract status | Header `generalRuleHeader`, `ruleOther`, `disputedSum`. With a contract that was not read, also the G1 or G2 sentence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `renderVerdict`                                                    |
| G4  | Returned > deposit (R4)                            | No verdict, no letter: `returnedAboveDeposit` and `disputedSum` in one reply.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `saveReturned`                                                     |

The owner approved the Russian header «Общее правило», `generalRuleContractFailed` and
`disputedSum` verbatim. `generalRuleNotLease` adapts the first clause of the approved sentence and
`returnedAboveDeposit` is a draft; all Ukrainian texts are translations in the same sense. None
of them has been reviewed by a lawyer. The approved sentences first addressed the person with the
formal «вы»; owner decision D42 switched them, like every other text of the bot, to the informal
«ты» (UK «ти») without changing their meaning. The Russian texts now read:
`generalRuleContractFailed` «Договор прочитать не удалось, поэтому ниже — общее правило закона, а
не вывод по твоему договору. Срок в твоём договоре может быть другим.»; `generalRuleNotLease`
«Это не похоже на договор аренды, поэтому ниже — общее правило закона, а не вывод по твоему
договору. Срок в твоём договоре может быть другим.»

## 3. The demand letter

The "Draft a letter to the landlord" button is offered under every verdict, whatever the strength
and the timing, general-rule verdicts included (`verdictKeyboard`, `deposit-flow.ts:233`). The letter is built when the button is
pressed, with that day's date and that day's overdue state (`deposit-flow.ts:600-632`,
`buildDemandLetter` `deposit.ts:324-374`). It is sent as one copyable block, with an intro
(`letterIntro`, or `letterIntroFilled` when names came from the contract) and `letterHowTo`.
Names and addresses read from the contract are deleted once the letter is built
(`deposit-flow.ts:633-639`), or by the daily cron after three days (`clearStaleLetterParties`,
`deposit-flow.ts:820-829`).

Template (`{…}` are values, `[…]` are placeholders the person fills in; `deposit.ts:333-373`):

```text
[Miejscowość], dnia {today, DD.MM.YYYY}

{tenant name from the contract | [Imię i nazwisko najemcy]}
[Adres do korespondencji]

{landlord name from the contract | [Imię i nazwisko lub nazwa wynajmującego]}
{landlord address from the contract | [Adres wynajmującego]}

WEZWANIE DO ZAPŁATY

Wzywam do zwrotu kaucji w kwocie {claim},00 zł, wpłaconej na podstawie umowy najmu lokalu położonego przy {flat address from the contract | [adres lokalu]}, w terminie 7 dni od dnia doręczenia niniejszego wezwania, na rachunek bankowy nr [numer rachunku].

Lokal został opróżniony i wydany w dniu {move-out date}. {TERM} {DEADLINE}

{REASON}

{CLOSING}

[Podpis]
```

## 6. Known limits

Facts about the implementation that bear on how far the verdict can be relied on. They are listed,
not resolved.

- **The contract is read by a language model.** `readContract` sends the files to Anthropic's
  `claude-opus-5-5` (`contract.ts:13`, `:175-212`) with fixed instructions (`contract.ts:104-116`)
  and a fixed output schema (`contract.ts:41-102`). The output always fits the schema, but its
  content is not checked against the document. The person sees the quoted clause (at most 300
  characters, `contract.ts:113`) for the return term, renovation and cleaning, so they can compare
  it with their contract; nothing else is shown.
- **Only three clauses decide anything.** The return term, the renovation rule (`tenant_must_renew`,
  `tenant_exempt`, `not_mentioned`) and the cleaning rule (`cleaning_required`, `not_mentioned`)
  (`contract.ts:20-35`). Other clauses (deductions, penalties, the condition of the flat, a
  najem okazjonalny or instytucjonalny regime) are not read. The contract type is read and stored
  but changes neither the verdict nor the letter (`deposit-flow.ts:320`). The deposit amount in the
  contract is read but not compared with the amount the person typed.
- **Partial reading.** An unreadable page counts as "not found" (`contract.ts:116`), so a clause on
  such a page becomes `not_mentioned` and the bot may say the contract has no return term of its
  own (D2). At most 10 files are read (C6); a PDF starts the reading at once, so later files are
  not included (C7). A return term outside 1 – 180 days or 1 – 6 months is discarded as a misread
  and treated as no term (`contract.ts:143-148`).
- **No case law.** The rules are a fixed table (table S). The only court decision named in the
  code is III C 224/22, in a comment. The code weighs no evidence, compares no amounts (for
  example, a withheld amount against the bills it covers) and does not consider how courts have
  treated a reason.
- **The strength depends only on the reason button, the protocol answer and three clauses.**
  `one` is treated like `both` (table S). The protocol answer changes the strength only for
  `damage`, and adds the `noProtocolNote` only for `wear`.
- **The deadline starts from the date the person typed** for "when did you move out and hand over
  the keys". The statute's wording in the letter is "od dnia opróżnienia lokalu"; the letter also
  says "Lokal został opróżniony i wydany w dniu {date}". The bot does not ask whether the two
  differ.
- **Dates are UTC days.** "Today" is the server's UTC date (`deposit.ts:86-88`, `:240`); between
  midnight and 01:00 or 02:00 Warsaw time it is still the previous day.
- **The deadline reminder arrives on the deadline day.** F1 stores the deadline itself
  (`deposit-flow.ts:680`), and the cron sends it that day, while `assessDeposit` counts that day
  as not yet overdue (`deposit.ts:241`, T2). The reminder text says the deadline "expired today".
- **The letter is offered in every scenario with a verdict,** including `bills` (lawful), the
  general-rule cases G1 – G3 and before the deadline (T1, T2). It always demands the whole `claim` (deposit − returned), also for `bills`, whose
  paragraph asks for the settlement and "the remaining part". The 7-day period runs from delivery
  of the letter, whatever the remaining deadline. The letter states that interest is due but
  computes no amount. Amounts are whole złoty.
- **Personal data are not asked for.** The letter leaves the tenant's address and the bank account
  as placeholders always, and names and addresses as placeholders unless the contract was read
  (`deposit.ts:318-323`, `:334-346`).
- **Explanations are in Russian and Ukrainian, the letter is in Polish.** The person may not be able
  to check the Polish letter.
