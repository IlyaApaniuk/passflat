## Notes

### The roommate ladder (2026-11-19)

E14 and E15 were written by Claude on 2026-11-19 from the owner's thresholds (D52) and the fit
report (`../research/roommate-fit-and-test-2026-11-19.md` §4). Every threshold is an
**Assumption** the owner may change.

- **E14, counting.** Profiles are persons with `tg_roommate_profile_created` in PostHog, because a
  profile deleted on request leaves no database row; one chat has at most one profile, so a person
  who deletes and re-creates counts once. "Active" and "per district" come from the database on
  the readout day (PRD 3 FR-42 holds the SQL). Owner-hours and manual minutes come from the
  posting log (PRD 3 FR-41); a week without a log entry is reported as missing, not as zero. The
  owner tests only on the dev bot, which runs without the PostHog key (D37), so production counts
  need no exclusion list.
- **E14, worked example** (the PRD 3 FR-42 golden case): 34 persons created a profile, 22 are
  active on 2027-01-18, Mokotów has 6 active profiles naming it, Wola 4, Ochota 3, Praga-Południe 2
  (3 districts reach 3), the owner logged 27.5 posting hours and on average 18 manual minutes a
  week. Profiles per owner-hour 34 ÷ 27.5 = 1.24. No stop rule fires (34 ≥ 15, 1.24 ≥ 1, 18 ≤ 30)
  and every go rule holds (34 ≥ 30, 22 ≥ 20, 3 districts): **go**.
- **E15, counting.** `tg_roommate_matched` fires once for each person of a new pair: `role=completer`
  for the person whose "Interested" completed it and `role=waiting` for the other, so pairs are
  the `completer` events and no event carries a partner or match id. The match rate uses events,
  not rows, so a pair who found each other and deleted their profiles still counts.
- **E15, qualifying persons (D74).** The match rate's denominator: persons whose profile was
  active at the switch (their last `tg_roommate_profile_created`, `_renewed`, `_expired` or
  `_deleted` event before it is a create or a renewal) or was created after it, and is at least
  14 days old at the readout. The earlier definition (created or renewed at least 14 days before
  the readout, not deleted before the switch) counted stage 1 profiles that had expired before the
  switch and were never shown; its rate is still reported, without a rule. Example: switch
  2027-01-08, readout 2027-01-22; a profile created 2026-12-01 expired on 2026-12-31 and does not
  qualify, though the earlier definition counts it; one created 2026-12-01 and renewed
  2026-12-29 qualifies; one created 2027-01-10 is under 14 days old and does not. With 30 persons
  under the earlier definition, 7 of them expired at the switch, plus 1 person who deleted a
  profile and created a new one before the switch: 24 qualifying persons, 8 with a match → 33 %,
  the 14-day rule holds; the earlier definition gives 8 ÷ 30 = 26.7 %, where no rule holds.
  `docs/analytics/activation-funnels.md` §8 holds the query and the cases.
- **E15, stop rule (D60).** "Qualifying persons" are the match-rate denominator above (D74);
  "browsing persons" have at least one `tg_roommate_cards_viewed` after the switch. Example:
  switch 2027-01-08, readout 2027-01-22; 24 qualifying persons, 3 with a match → 12.5 % < 15 % →
  stop; 22 browsing persons and no `completer` event → stop. Outcomes count persons, so both
  people of one pair may answer.
- **E14, extension (D59).** A readout between stop and go extends stage 1 once to 2027-02-28
  with the same rules and the switch off; the extended readout applies the same stop and go
  rules, and its gap is the owner's call on that day.
- **Season.** Both experiments run in the December–January trough; a null result is weaker
  evidence than one in August–September (research 2026-11-19), and the readouts say so.

## Value metrics without a threshold

Reported in readouts, never used as a go/stop rule.

- **Deposit value (D44).** Persons (`tg_<chatId>`) with `tg_deposit_letter_sent` (their own
  mark) → of them, the last `tg_deposit_outcome` = `full`, `partial`, `none`, or no answer, in
  the 30-day window of `../analytics/activation-funnels.md` §2.5. Counts, not percentages, while
  samples are small. A letter is not money returned.

## Readouts

PostHog readouts are saved in `reviews/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-metrics.md` by the `metrics-review`
skill. A readout updates the "Result" column only with a date range, the query used and whether
it counts events, persons or database rows.
