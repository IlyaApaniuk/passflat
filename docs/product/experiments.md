## Proposed experiments (2026-11-19)

E10–E12 were written by Claude in the night run of 2026-11-18/19 from
`reviews/2026-11-18-metrics.md` and `reviews/2026-11-18-direction-review.md`. Every threshold is
an **Assumption** until the owner confirms it.

- **E10** refines E6 (same channel, plus a source per link and a paid next-step probe). If the
  owner adopts E10, E6 should be marked Stopped or replaced; that is the owner's call. Starting
  E10 promotes the deposit flow, which the BRD ties to a Polish lawyer review of the rules and
  the letter (A14, BRD §13); the owner decides whether to start before that review. Readout:
  persons, not events; the owner's own test chats are excluded; a funnel with a 14-day window
  broken down by `entry_source` of its first step.
- **E11** tests the owner's roommate hypothesis in its cheapest honest form. Room cost figures
  may only come from Passflat data at N ≥ 5 or from dated, cited sources (CLAUDE.md data
  honesty rules). The build is not in PRD 2; it needs a small follow-up item.
- **E12** compares Search Console weeks, not PostHog sessions, so it does not depend on the
  landing-capture fix. The title rewrite is not in PRD 2; the guide prompt is (PRD 2 FR-7).

## Readouts

PostHog readouts are saved in `reviews/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-metrics.md` by the `metrics-review`
skill. A readout updates the "Result" column only with a date range, the query used and whether
it counts events, persons or database rows.
