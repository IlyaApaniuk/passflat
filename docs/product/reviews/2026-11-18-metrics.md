## Verdict

1. **The PostHog funnel is broken at the first step since 2026-07-28.** From 2026-08-01 to
   2026-11-18, 1,172 of 1,364 visits (86 %) have no `$pageview` at all; every one of them is a
   single-page visit. Google visits seen in PostHog match Search Console clicks almost exactly
   (September 33 vs 28, October 29 vs 30, November 17 vs 17), so the SDK loads and sends
   `$web_vitals` and `$pageleave`, but the landing `$pageview` and other mount-time events
   (`building_detail_viewed`, last seen 2026-09-24) are lost. The likely mechanism, to verify by
   a test: `PostHogPageView` and page components capture in a child `useEffect`, which React runs
   before the parent `useEffect` that calls `posthog.init`, so the first capture of every full
   page load is dropped. The "39 pageviews in 30 days" figure is an artefact of this, not the
   traffic. PRD 1 package A builds on `$pageview` and needs this fixed first.
2. **Real traffic is small and mostly search.** 152 visits in the last 8 full weeks, of which
   54 from Google (Search Console: 54 clicks in the same weeks). Threads and Telegram brought
   single visits after August.
3. **Nothing converts.** In the last 8 full weeks: 2 `cost_form_started`, 0
   `cost_report_submitted` (server-side, reliable), 0 `address_checked`. Since 2026-07-01 the
   whole site produced 2 server-side cost reports from non-test persons. No
   `affiliate_link_clicked`, `city_notify_subscribed`, `district_notify_subscribed` or `tg_*`
   event exists in the project at all.
4. **Search demand by page:** building pages bring the most clicks (49 of 116, 42 %), the
   roommate and sublet pages the most clicks per page (30, 26 %; `/warsaw/roommate` alone 25),
   the blog the most impressions (3,230 of 7,904 page impressions, 41 %) at the lowest CTR
   (0.71 %). Clicks are flat at 4–9 a week since September while the average position improved
   from ~13 to ~6.
5. **The owner's roommate hypothesis:** roommate search is the clearest _intent_ in search (12
   of the 15 visible query clicks) and the strongest single page, but not the majority of
   demand (26 % of clicks), and on the site it converts to nothing: 41 roommate or sublet
   landings since 2026-08-01, 0 listing views, 0 contacts, 0 saved interests, 0 form starts;
   10 of them saw the district waitlist (an empty offer list). Details in the Search section
   and H2.

## Levers

1. **Fix landing capture before anything else.** Initialise PostHog before the first capture
   (for example in `instrumentation-client.ts` or at module load) or queue captures until
   `loaded`, and add a test that a capture issued on mount is not dropped. Expected: visits with
   `$pageview` from 14 % to above 90 %, `building_detail_viewed` back on building landings.
   Cheapest test: unit test now, then compare `$pageview` sessions with `$web_vitals` sessions in
   the first full week after deploy. Without it, PRD 1 E8 and E9 cannot be read.
2. **Give roommate searchers an answer the page can actually deliver.** `/warsaw/roommate` got
   1,409 Google impressions in 12 months at position 8.8 and shows an empty offer list. A page that
   answers "how much does a room cost in my district, what is included, how to check the
   address, how to get the deposit back" and offers one concrete next step (bot or saved
   interest) can be measured within two weeks. Expected: roommate landings with a useful action
   from 0 of 41 to a few a week; CTR from 1.8 % towards 3 % with a sharper title. Cheapest test:
   copy and title change plus one CTA, read by the PRD 1 events.
3. **Rewrite titles and snippets of the five blog pages on page 1 with CTR below 1 %**
   (meters, hidden costs EN, no-agent EN, occasional lease EN, cesja). They hold 2,000
   impressions a year; at 2 % CTR that is ~25 more clicks a year, small but free. Cheapest test:
   rewrite, then compare four weeks in Search Console.
