# Structure, navigation, information architecture and the thesis

Audited 2026-11-19 on branch `chore/agent-harness` (read-only). "Code" = the branch as the future
site; "live" = https://passflat.com today (pre-PRD-1 build, `NEXT_PUBLIC_TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME`
unset). Paths are repository-relative; routes omit the locale prefix.

## Verdict

The site does not yet read as "find someone to rent with and see what it costs per person". Every
framing surface still tells the 2025 story "costs, listings, documents": the home title and
description (`meta.homeTitle`), the hero, the footer tagline and Product column, the home "How it
works" tabs, `/how-it-works`, `/about` and `/help` all describe a listings marketplace (lease
takeover, roommate listings, sublet, promoted listings from 39 PLN) with cost data on the side.
Nothing on the site mentions the Telegram bot except two env-gated CTAs (the checker result and the
roommate block), and no cost surface (header, `/warsaw` hub, building pages with 42 % of Google
clicks, `/costs`, calculator, blog) links to `/warsaw/roommate` or the bot. The one page built for
the thesis, `/warsaw/roommate`, is still the frozen listings shell: type tabs to `/replacement` and
`/sublet`, "0 listings", filters, an e-mail waitlist and "Post a listing"; the PRD 3 "find a
flatmate" block renders only after the visitor clicks the third role button, and only when the bot
username is configured. The frozen listings are linked from the footer, the hero pills, the "for
whom" section, the 404 page, the building page and the sitemap, so they are promoted, not frozen.
The good news: the PRD 1 honesty fixes are on the branch (live still shows "zweryfikowane oferty",
a 77 % deposit tile and "сколько платят жильцы"), hreflang and the locale switcher work, and the
roommate URL that already ranks (position 8.8) can stay. The fixes below are mostly copy and link
changes; the two that matter most are a thesis section on the home page with a nav entry (ST-1,
ST-6) and showing the flatmate block by default on the roommate page (ST-2).
