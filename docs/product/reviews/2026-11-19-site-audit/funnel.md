# Funnel and copy

Audit of branch `chore/agent-harness` (code = the future site, PRD 1 + PRD 3 built, not deployed) against the thesis (D48). Live state (https://passflat.com, read-only GET on 2026-11-19) is quoted only to show what a visitor sees today. "Code" = this branch; "live" = production.

## Verdict

The branch has exactly one working path into the roommate bot, and it is hidden: on `/warsaw/roommate` a visitor first sees the frozen listings UI (type tabs "Lease Takeover | Roommate Search | Temporary Sublet", "0 listings", sort, filters), must tap a third role button, and only then sees the "Looking for someone to rent with?" block with the `mate_web_roommate` button (two clicks to the bot; nothing at all appears if `NEXT_PUBLIC_TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME` is unset). Every other entry the thesis names (home, building pages, checker, guides, costs overview, calculator) has no mention of finding someone to rent with and no link to the roommate entry; the home page, how-it-works, about and help still describe a cesja marketplace with "three ways to find your next home", and the roommate page's Google snippet promises "Flatmate listings … rooms in shared flats" that have not existed since June. Honesty copy after PRD 1 is much better than live (the "actually pay" / "we'll tell you the moment they appear" strings are gone in code), but a "real costs / the full truth" family survives in 16 keys outside the honesty test's list, and the four locales disagree on what languages the templates come in. The biggest lever for stage 1 is not new design: show the flatmate block by default on the roommate page, rewrite its title and snippet to the thesis, add one roommate entry to the home page and the building page, and put a per-person figure next to the block.

## Findings

### FU-1. The roommate page opens on the frozen product; the bot block is behind a tap

- **What:** The first screen of `/warsaw/roommate` is the listings UI (type tabs, "0 listings", sort, map, quick filters); the "Looking for someone to rent with?" block renders only after `roommate_intent_selected {intent: find_flatmate}` (`showsFlatmateBlock` in `src/lib/roommate-roles.ts`), while the e-mail capture and "Post a listing" show by default.
- **Where:** `src/components/listings/listings-page-client.tsx:256–335, 385–387`; `src/lib/roommate-roles.ts` `showsFlatmateBlock`; key `listings.listingsInCity`.
- **Evidence:** code renders `listings.listingsInCity` ("<b>{count}</b> listings" / "ofert" / "объявлений" / "оголошень") with `count = 0` above the roles; live PL page text begins "Przejęcie najmu | Szukam współlokatora | Tymczasowy podnajem | 0 | ofert". Metrics: 41 roommate landings since August, 0 useful actions.
- **Why it matters:** Stage 1 inflow from the only page Google already sends people to (25 clicks, 1,409 impressions); every extra tap on a 90 % single-page audience is a drop.
- **Proposed change:** (1) `showsFlatmateBlock` returns true when `intent` is `undefined` or `find_flatmate` (hidden only after `find_room` / `offer_room`); (2) on roommate, hide the count line when `cityOfferCount === 0`; (3) render `RoommateRoleSelector` above `ListingTypeTabs` on roommate so the question comes before the frozen tabs. Update `roommate-role-selector.test.ts` / `room-money-placement.test.ts`. Hiding the type tabs on roommate is "Open: owner decision" (D5 keeps listings reachable).
- **Size:** S. **Rank:** 1.
